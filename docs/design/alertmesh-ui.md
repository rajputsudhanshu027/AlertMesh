# AlertMesh browser UI direction

## Direction contract

**THESIS:** The app is a working field folio for keeping a person's Places and official Alerts legible at a glance; it avoids the generic three-column monitoring console.

**OWN-WORLD:** Cool green-gray field paper, deep evergreen ink, restrained safety amber, and fine ruled divisions make the UI feel like a dependable field reference. Status uses words and shape as well as color. Maps and source records stay crisp and functional.

**STORY:** People see the condition of their saved Places, scan matching Alerts, and open a record for affected area, guidance, source freshness, and delivery status. Account access and Place management feel like pages in the same folio.

**FIRST VIEWPORT:** A compact evergreen masthead sits above a broad field-paper canvas. The title and last-checked time lead. Source freshness spans the page, followed by a narrow Place index beside a broad Inbox ledger; the selected Alert record opens as a full-width folio spread below it.

**FORM:** Field folio, selected by the user. One responsive document flow replaces the current dashboard styling; desktop pairs the Place index with the Inbox and opens Alert detail beneath the ledger, while narrow screens stack the index, Inbox, and record in task order.

**FINISH:** unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
